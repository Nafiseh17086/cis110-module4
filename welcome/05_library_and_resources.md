# Library and University Resources

## Northwestern University Library

The library supports distance-learning students fully. Contact the Distance
Learning Librarian, **Tracy Coyne**, at 312-503-6617 or
tracy-coyne@northwestern.edu. Remote access to library databases requires
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

## AccessibleNU

This course is designed to be welcoming to, accessible to, and usable by
everyone. If you encounter any part of the course that is not accessible to
you, please tell me right away.

Northwestern and AccessibleNU provide reasonable accommodations to students
with disabilities and other conditions requiring accommodation. If you need
accommodations for the two timed checkpoints in Modules 5 and 8, contact
AccessibleNU and your instructor at least one week in advance.

## SPS Student Services

Each program has an assigned adviser who can help with academic planning,
policies, and any situation affecting your studies — including falling
behind, or family or medical issues. Contact:
**spsacademicadvising@northwestern.edu**.
