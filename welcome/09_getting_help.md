# Getting Help

Being stuck is a normal part of programming. What matters is getting unstuck
efficiently.

## Where to go

| Where | Best for | How fast |
|---|---|---|
| **Q&A discussion** (Canvas) | Anything about labs, setup, or course logistics | Classmates and the teaching team — usually fastest |
| **Office hours** | Working through a problem together, live | See [About the Instructor](03_about_the_instructor.md) |
| **Email your instructor** | Anything personal, private, or about grades | Within the stated response time |
| **SPS Learning Studios** | Extra support with common tools and topics — self-paced content with an instructor available | Self-enroll free via the SPS Academic Services page |
| **SPS academic advising** | Falling behind, dropping, or personal circumstances | spsacademicadvising@northwestern.edu |

## How to ask a question that gets answered quickly

Include three things:

1. **What you were trying to do** — "Exercise 6, asking the user for miles."
2. **What you expected** — "It should print 16.09."
3. **What actually happened** — paste the **full** error message as text, not a
   screenshot.

Most people find the answer themselves while writing those three lines down.
That is not a coincidence — it is debugging.

## Before posting a setup problem

Run `python setup/verify_environment.py` and paste its full output. That one
step answers most of the questions we would otherwise have to ask you.
