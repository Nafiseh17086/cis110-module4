# Discussions

## Discussion: Q&A — ungraded, open all quarter

Ask anything about the course, the labs, or setup. If you know the answer to a
classmate's question, answer it — explaining something is one of the best ways
to learn it. Please do not post complete solutions to graded labs.

## Discussion: Water Cooler — ungraded, open all quarter

Off-topic, social, and everything else: projects you're working on, interesting
articles, study groups, or introducing your pets.

## Discussion: Introductions — 20 points (Welcome module)

**Initial post**, by the end of the first Thursday at 11:59 p.m. CT. Tell us:

1. Your name, where you are, and your program of study.
2. Why you're taking CIS 110, and what you hope to be able to build by the end.
3. Your honest starting point: have you programmed before? Have you used AI
   tools like ChatGPT or Claude? There is no wrong answer — the course is
   designed for people starting from zero.
4. One thing outside work or school that you care about.

**Replies**, by Sunday at 11:59 p.m. CT: respond to at least **two** classmates.
Find something you have in common, or ask them a genuine question.
