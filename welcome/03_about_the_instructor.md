## A little about me

I am a machine learning scientist with a Ph.D. in Engineering. I am currently
a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Cell and Developmental Biology at
Northwestern's Feinberg School of Medicine, and before that I spent more than
five years as a data scientist in industry. I build production machine
learning systems and agentic AI pipelines, and I hold AWS certifications in
cloud architecture and AI.

I came to programming as a tool for solving problems in other fields — which
is probably how many of you are coming to it too. That shapes how I teach:
code is a way of thinking clearly, not a subject to memorize.

**One thing to focus on that isn't obvious:** learn to *predict* what code will
do before you run it. It feels slow at first. It is the single skill that
makes everything else in this course — including working with AI — easier.
